# Northstar Support Co. — Support Policy Knowledge Base
Sections are keyed by ticket category. The `## header` names must match the categories in state.py exactly, or policy_lookup will miss.

## billing
*   Refunds are issued to the original payment method and take 3-5 business days to appear.
*   Duplicate-charge refunds are issued based on the customer's report; support does not independently verify against transaction records before refunding.
*   Duplicate charges are refunded in full, no questions asked, within 60 days of the charge date.
*   Subscription cancellations take effect at the end of the current billing period. We do not prorate partial months.
*   Customers may change their payment method at any time from Account Settings. We cannot change it on their behalf.
*   Refunds over $100 require supervisor approval before being issued.
   
## technical
*   Login failures: first ask the customer to reset their password using the "Forgot password" link. If that fails, the account may be locked after five failed attempts, which clears automatically after 30 minutes.
*   Sync errors between devices are usually resolved by signing out and back in on the affected device.
*   We support the current and previous major version of iOS, Android, Chrome, Safari, and Firefox. We do not troubleshoot unsupported versions.
*   Known outages are posted at status.northstar.example. If an outage is active, tell the customer and give the posted estimate. Do not invent an ETA.
*   We cannot access a customer's account contents for privacy reasons.

## returns
*   Items may be returned within 14 days of delivery for a full refund.
*   Return shipping is free if the item arrived damaged, defective, or was the wrong item sent. Otherwise the customer pays return shipping.
*   Damaged items: ask for a photo before issuing a replacement. Replacements ship within 2 business days of the photo being received.
*   Opened consumable items cannot be returned.
*   Exchanges are processed as a return plus a new order. We cannot do a direct swap.
* After approval, a prepaid return label with be generated and emailed to the customer. Ensure the email includes the label, packing instructions, and required return-by date. 

## other
*   If a ticket does not fit billing, technical, or returns, do not attempt to resolve it.
*   Acknowledge the customer, tell them it is being routed to a specialist, and set expectations of a reply within one business day.
